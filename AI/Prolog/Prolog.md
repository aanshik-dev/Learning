## 🐦‍🔥 What is Prolog ?

Prolog = Programming in Logic

- A declarative (descriptive) language, not procedural
- You describe the world by stating:
  - Facts (things that are true)
  - Rules (relationships between facts)
- Then you ask queries (questions) about this described world

| Concept | Analogy                                                                  |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Facts   | "Elephants are bigger than horses"                                       |
| Rules   | "If X is bigger than Y, and Y is bigger than Z, then X is bigger than Z" |
| Queries | "Is an elephant bigger than a donkey?"                                   |
